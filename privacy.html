Privacy policy

Alfred, a private single-user assistant. Last updated 5 October 2026.

Who uses Alfred

One person: its owner. Alfred is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What Alfred accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, Alfred reads the owner's own Google Calendar events and Gmail messages, can save drafts, and can create calendar events or send an email only after the owner approves that exact item.

Where the data goes

Nowhere. Alfred runs on the owner's own computer. Calendar and mail data are processed on that computer to produce summaries for the owner and are not uploaded to any server operated by Alfred, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The sign-in token is stored in a protected file on the owner's computer.

Google user data

Alfred's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the assistant's features to the owner, is not transferred to others, and is not used to train generalized models.

Retention and deletion

Summaries and a log of the assistant's actions are kept on the owner's computer and can be deleted by the owner at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions go to the owner through the support address shown on the Google sign-in screen for Alfred.
